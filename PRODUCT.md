# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Privacy-conscious technical users working on macOS, Windows, or Linux. They understand networking terms such as providers, SOCKS binds, PAC, TUN, latency, and diagnostics, and want direct control without account or onboarding overhead.

## Product Purpose

Bulletproof is a local-first desktop VPN client for connecting through WARP, Gool, or Psiphon. It should make the current routing state immediately legible, expose precise configuration and diagnostics, and keep identities and operational data on the user's machine.

## Brand Personality

Technical, restrained, and trustworthy. It should feel maintained by practitioners: direct language, useful density, plain materials, and visible system state. The interface should resemble a well-made open-source desktop utility rather than a commercial SaaS product.

## Anti-references

Avoid the existing glossy amber-on-black interface, oversized toggle, emoji-led navigation, glowing gradients, glass effects, pill-heavy status displays, interchangeable card grids, promotional copy, and generic AI-generated dashboard styling. Avoid making the app resemble a landing page, consumer subscription product, or brand showcase.

## Design Principles

1. Show system state before decoration.
2. Prefer native utility patterns over product-page patterns.
3. Keep controls explicit, compact, and close to their consequences.
4. Use technical detail as evidence, not as visual noise.
5. Make every label and sentence earn its space.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use clear contrast, semantic controls, visible keyboard focus, and reduced-motion support. Keep labels concise and avoid excessive descriptive text. Do not hide essential state behind color alone.
